MYM Engineering Personal Assistants

MYM Laboratory & Medical Supply, Inc. Engineering owns and manages multiple personal assistants on company-controlled, self-hosted infrastructure. Each assistant helps authorized business users manage selected Google Calendar and Gmail workflows through explicit, user-directed requests.

Purpose

An assistant connects to an authorized business user’s Google account using OAuth. Access is used only to perform tasks requested by that user, such as reviewing email, checking schedules, and managing calendar events.

Data principles

  • Business use: The assistants are available only to authorized MYM business users and are not offered to the public.
  • User control: Authorized business users can revoke Google access at any time.
  • No advertising: Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.
  • Local operation: OAuth credentials are stored on company-controlled, self-hosted infrastructure.

Google API disclosure

MYM Engineering’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
