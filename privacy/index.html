Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Taro Personal Assistant (“Taro” or “the App”) accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google user data.

1. Scope and operator

Taro is a private, self-hosted, single-user application operated by and solely for its owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not support accounts for other users.

2. Google data Taro may access

Depending on the permissions granted by the owner, Taro may access:

  • Gmail: email messages, threads, labels, and related metadata.
  • Gmail actions: sending messages or modifying mailbox state only when the owner explicitly requests and authorizes the action.
  • Google Calendar: calendars, events, schedules, attendees, and related metadata.
  • Calendar actions: creating, updating, or deleting events only when the owner explicitly requests and approves the action.
  • Basic account information: information necessary for OAuth authorization and account identification.

3. How Google data is used

Google user data is used only to:

  • answer the owner’s questions about email and calendars;
  • summarize, search, organize, or prepare information requested by the owner;
  • perform owner-directed Gmail or Calendar operations;
  • maintain and troubleshoot the private integration; and
  • protect the security and reliability of the App.

Taro does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling for ads, credit decisions, or sale to third parties.

4. Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner’s self-hosted machine. Relevant Google data, tool results, summaries, or instructions may also appear in local application sessions, logs, notes, or backups controlled by the owner.

When necessary to fulfill an owner-requested task, limited relevant content may be transmitted to the AI model, hosting, or infrastructure providers configured by the owner. Such processing is limited to providing the requested functionality and is not authorized for advertising or sale.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Taro does not sell Google user data. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties. It may be processed by service providers configured by the owner only as necessary to operate the App, comply with the owner’s explicit request, maintain security, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained until the owner revokes access or removes them. Locally stored sessions, logs, notes, and backups are retained under the owner’s own retention settings. The owner can delete local data and credentials directly from the self-hosted system.

7. Security

The App uses Google OAuth rather than storing the owner’s Google password. Credentials and local data are protected using operating-system access controls and the security measures configured on the owner’s private infrastructure. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Owner control and revocation

The owner can review or revoke Taro’s access through Google Account connections. The owner can also remove the locally stored OAuth token and related data from the self-hosted machine.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

Taro’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner through the owner’s GitHub profile or the support email displayed on Taro’s Google OAuth consent screen.
