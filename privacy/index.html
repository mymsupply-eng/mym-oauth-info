Effective September 19, 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the personal assistants managed by MYM Laboratory & Medical Supply, Inc. Engineering (each, an “App”) access, use, store, and protect Google user data.

1. Scope and operator

MYM Laboratory & Medical Supply, Inc. Engineering owns and manages multiple Apps on company-controlled, self-hosted infrastructure. The Apps are for authorized MYM business users only; they are not offered as a public service and do not support public accounts.

2. Google data an App may access

Depending on the permissions granted by an authorized business user, an App may access:

  • Gmail: email messages, threads, labels, and related metadata.
  • Gmail actions: sending messages or modifying mailbox state only when the authorized business user explicitly requests and authorizes the action.
  • Google Calendar: calendars, events, schedules, attendees, and related metadata.
  • Calendar actions: creating, updating, or deleting events only when the authorized business user explicitly requests and approves the action.
  • Basic account information: information necessary for OAuth authorization and account identification.

3. How Google data is used

Google user data is used only to:

  • answer an authorized business user’s questions about email and calendars;
  • summarize, search, organize, or prepare information requested by an authorized business user;
  • perform Gmail or Calendar operations directed by an authorized business user;
  • maintain and troubleshoot the private integration; and
  • protect the security and reliability of the App.

MYM Engineering does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling for ads, credit decisions, or sale to third parties.

4. Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored on company-controlled, self-hosted infrastructure. Relevant Google data, tool results, summaries, or instructions may also appear in local application sessions, logs, notes, or backups controlled by MYM Engineering.

When necessary to fulfill a task requested by an authorized business user, limited relevant content may be transmitted to the AI model, hosting, or infrastructure providers configured by MYM Engineering. Such processing is limited to providing the requested functionality and is not authorized for advertising or sale.

5. Sharing and disclosure

MYM Engineering does not sell Google user data. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties. It may be processed by service providers configured by the company only as necessary to operate an App, comply with an authorized business user’s explicit request, maintain security, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained until an authorized business user revokes access or MYM Engineering removes them. Locally stored sessions, logs, notes, and backups are retained under MYM Engineering’s applicable retention settings. Authorized personnel can delete local data and credentials from the self-hosted system.

7. Security

Each App uses Google OAuth rather than storing a business user’s Google password. Credentials and local data are protected using operating-system access controls and the security measures configured on MYM Engineering’s company-controlled infrastructure. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Business-user control and revocation

An authorized business user can review or revoke an App’s access through Google Account connections. MYM Engineering can also remove locally stored OAuth tokens and related data from the self-hosted infrastructure.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

MYM Engineering’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to MYM Engineering through the support email displayed on the applicable App’s Google OAuth consent screen.
